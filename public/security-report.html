Is the platform ready and safe for the market?

A plain-English briefing for the ADMA / Mediaserv team — use this when exhibitors, sponsors, or partners ask how we handle their data and payments.

Prepared 5 August 2026 Internal use → external talking points

The short version

Four things worth saying with confidence

No jargon needed — these are simply true today, and they're the things that matter most to anyone trusting us with their business.

💳

We're built to never touch card details. Payments run through Paynow, Zimbabwe's established payment gateway, designed so card and mobile-money numbers never reach our servers — Paynow's own merchant verification is in its final stages, expected to complete any day now.

🔒

Bank-grade encryption, everywhere. Data is scrambled both while it travels to you and while it's stored — the same standard used by banks and major online platforms worldwide.

🔑

Admin logins need a second step. A password alone isn't enough for anyone with organiser-level access — a phone-based code is required too.

💾

Your data is backed up every second, and we've proven it. We didn't just switch on backups — we actually deleted a test copy of the system and restored it successfully, on purpose, to check the safety net really works.

☁️

Hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS). AWS is the cloud infrastructure company behind Netflix, Airbnb, and a large share of the world's biggest online platforms — we're not running on a spare server in a back office; we're on the same infrastructure the internet's biggest names trust.

How we compare to international standards

We measured ourselves against a checklist major technology companies use

It's called the CAIQ — a 263-point security checklist published by the Cloud Security Alliance, an international, non-profit standards body. Large technology companies use it to assess themselves and their partners, so it's a fair, recognised yardstick rather than something we invented to make ourselves look good.

Security on ADMA Digital hasn't been a single push — it's been built up in layers as the platform matured: secure logins and access control, then real payment handling, and most recently a full, methodical review against this international checklist to find and close whatever was still missing.

Locked down today

What's genuinely solid right now

  • ✓ Done

    Your data, backed up continuously. Every change is captured automatically, with a tested, working restore process — not just a checkbox someone ticked.

  • ✓ Done

    A written plan for "what if something goes wrong." Who gets notified, how fast, and what we tell affected exhibitors — decided in advance, not improvised during a crisis.

  • ✓ Done

    Strict access boundaries. One exhibitor's data is never visible to another; staff only see what their role requires.

  • ✓ Done

    A full activity trail behind the scenes, not just on the website. Every administrative action on the underlying hosting account is now permanently logged, not only what happens on-screen.

  • ✓ Done

    Automatic traffic-flood protection. The platform now rejects abusive bursts of traffic before they can slow things down for real visitors.

  • ✓ Done

    You can download your own data, any time. A one-click export of everything we hold about your account — no waiting on an email request.

  • ✓ Done

    Running on two servers instead of one, in different physical locations. A standby server is live in a second location, so a single hardware failure can't take the whole platform down.

Waiting on scale, on purpose

What's still growing — and why that's the responsible order

Nothing below is a secret weakness — it's the same sequence most fast-growing platforms follow: fix everything that's cheap and fast first, then invest in the expensive, ongoing protections once the traffic and revenue justify carrying that cost every month.

◐ Planned

An independent, paid security test — outside experts trying to break in. This is the single most valuable next step, and the kind of evidence a serious partner actually wants to see. It's scoped and quote-ready; commissioning it is a budget decision timed to a major growth moment (like the 2027 event) rather than the platform's day-to-day engineering.

◐ Scales with the team

Formal HR security processes — background checks, signed agreements. These genuinely only make sense once hiring grows beyond the small founding team who already know and trust each other. Building this now would be paperwork for its own sake.

Bottom line for exhibitor conversations: ADMA Digital already has the real, load-bearing protections in place — encrypted data, verified backups, controlled access, a written incident plan, and a full account activity trail. What's left is the kind of investment that scales with success, not a shortcut we're hoping nobody notices. We're actively closing the gap against an internationally recognised standard, and we can show our work.
